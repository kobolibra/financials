# FINANCIALS IN VISUALS · Research & Publication Skill v8.2-RC3

## The two independent research foundations (MANDATORY)

**Pillar A — financial visual storytelling:** read `research/01_APP_ECONOMY_INSIGHTS_RESEARCH.md` before selecting figures. It reconstructs from first-party public App Economy Insights posts the business-first research flow and **full** graphic grammar: income flows, signed P&L waterfalls, balance composition, net income→CFO, financing and cash, FCF and capital allocation, multi-quarter revenue, margins, debt, price vs fundamentals, valuation spectrum, peers. Its source links must be retained; the visuals must be original, not copied from AEI.

**Pillar B — institutional A-share fundamentals:** read `research/02_ASHARE_INSTITUTIONAL_RESEARCH.md` before conducting the company analysis. The applicable CSRC rules, Chinese accounting standards, industry and exchange disclosures determine all facts, materiality, analytical ratios and caveats. *CAS, reporting periods and consolidation scope take precedence over beautiful graphics.*

Never treat these pillars as interchangeable. A well-designed chart is not proof of an investment claim. A well-supported financial table is not yet a compelling visual publication.

## Mandatory next-level research and editorial system (2026-11-01)

The **independent research foundations** are now implemented in two additional first-party source-based manuals:

- **Graphic / presentation research**: [03_VISUAL_EDITORIAL_SYSTEM.md](research/03_VISUAL_EDITORIAL_SYSTEM.md). This separately researches public App Economy Insights methods and the Financial Times Visual Vocabulary (distinct source) and specifies 22 accounting-aware chart recipes, two-speed reading, layout and typography, zero baselines, signed bridges, mobile and per-figure screenshot review.
- **Institutional A-share fundamentals / forensic work**: [04_ASHARE_FORENSIC_DEEP_DIVE.md](research/04_ASHARE_FORENSIC_DEEP_DIVE.md). This separately researches CSRC 2025 **annual AND half-year** disclosure requirements, MOF CAS-14 and 2025 annual-report accounting guidance, 2026 regulatory accounting failure themes and Shenzhen GEM communication disclosures. It defines 20 analyst examination stages and exact reconciliation rules.

**Mandatory analytical split**: Public company filing-only note vs fully funded multi-period institutional equity research. The latter requires external dated 8–12Q history, industry/customer/peer evidence, market prices and valuation inputs. One H1 PDF cannot honestly support them. Do not claim AEI-affiliated or paywalled templates were inspected. Use original visual composition, not copied branded graphics.

**Story-level editorial contract before figures**: (1) company role/value chain; (2) investment-relevant question; (3) accounting mechanism; (4) relative comparison and explicit reporting periods; (5) balance-sheet/cash rebuttal; (6) strongest alternative cause; (7) what next disclosure would falsify the lead. A chart **must** map to one question and one data relationship. No mandatory number of charts, no auto-approval for 6, 9, 11 or 20 images.

**Mandatory figure brief**: `chart_id; analytical_question; take_away; fact_ids; issuer; report_version; physical_pdf_page; period_or_date; consolidation; units; original_vs_derived; numerator; denominator; formula; reconciliation; graphic_family; domain_and_zero; color_encoding; source_caption; alternative_hypothesis; accessibility; mobile_policy; reviewer; publication_gate`.

**Figure selection**: 22 documented recipes cover growth, segment mix, GP contributors, signed P&L, ΔGP, quantity-ASP-mix only with reported inputs, revenue and margin timelines, debt, cash and credit ladders, indirect CFO, cash movements, FCF, asset composition, AR+inventory stocks, concentration, capital return, ROIC, price/fundamental indexing, valuation and genuine peers. Chart families are selected by *change-over-time, magnitude, deviation, stock composition, part-to-whole, ranking, flow and correlation*, not the author's favorite diagram. A large Sankey can be the lead figure but never substitutes for the rest of the analysis.

**Adversarial accounting checks**: earnings vs CFO uses consolidated NP; balance stocks vs signed CFO movements are not interchangeable; restricted cash vs cash equivalents differ; product revenue divided by total shipped units is an aggregate realization proxy, NOT same-SKU ASP; prior adjusted EPS can differ after bonus stock; M&A/restatement comparables must be documented; FX P&L != cash flow FX != OCI. Missing data are not zero and not a forced `other` bucket.

**Design and UX blocking tests**: per-chart complete screenshot critique (1440/768/390), semantic zero-baseline checks, visible axes and precise direct labels, balanced Chinese hero line breaking (no one-character final line), accessible static narrative, annotations close to graphics, no report in homepage iframe or `document.write`. Every company has its **own real independent HTML route**.

**Real-world proof of method**: standalone industry-chain report `/optical-2026h1.html` uses three separate user-uploaded original 2026H1 PDFs. It does **not** rank suppliers with module makers as same economics. Six distinct charts: zero-origin revenue growth, margin dumbbells, CFO/consolidated-NP conversion, signed zero-origin simple FCF, changes in working asset share of total assets, 100%-asset composition. Reproducible inputs must supply original PDFs and normalized ledgers to [nextlevel engine](nextlevel/engine/build_comparative.py). A GitHub commit does not prove Cloudflare live response.

**Separate pass/fail gates**: `PDF_HASH`, `PRIMARY_PAGE_TRACE`, `ACCOUNTING_EXACT`, `CAUSAL_LANGUAGE_REVIEW`, `RED_TEAM`, `CHART_SEMANTICS`, `TYPOGRAPHY_AND_FIGURE_VISUAL`, `BROWSER_NAV`, `GITHUB_BLOB`, `LIVE_HTTP`, `INDEPENDENT_FINANCIAL_REVIEWER`. Unknown is `NOT_CHECKED`, never success. These gates cannot be averaged into a '98% ready' claim.

## Cross-sector source-first upgrade / v8.1-RC1 — 2026-11-02 (MANDATORY)

**This is a candidate methodology update on a validation branch, not a proven 'best-in-class' release.** It is grounded in the 2026H1 original PDF cases of Kingsoft Office (688111), Kweichow Moutai (600519) and Midea (000333); see repository `PROJECT_STATE.md`. The source-byte acquisition, 92 selected fact/page matches and 11 targeted arithmetic checks are not an independent exhaustive three-statement audit.

1. **PDF ACQUISITION HARD GATE.** Never begin a source-certified report from public extracted web text alone. Obtain the issuer's original PDF bytes; capture canonical exchange/regulator URL, full-file SHA-256, byte size, PDF magic, physical page count, issuer identity, reporting period, amendment date and audit status. An official PDF whose graphical cover does not expose its ticker to text extraction must not be rejected solely by that one check: corroborate legal name/year, full report and visual cover. On blocked container networks, the separately tested GitHub Actions `.github/workflows/verify-2026h1-pdfs.yml` on the validation branch can retrieve and archive original bytes. Persist sources beyond short-lived CI artifacts. On failure: `NO_ORIGINAL_BYTES`, not PASS.
2. **PAGE+UNIT TWO-STAGE AUDIT.** Stage A automatically checks original numeric strings on the **physical PDF page** and reconciles normalized yuan with reported yuan, 千元 or 万元. Stage B an analyst verifies the table row/column heading, consolidation, time period, sign, audit status, notes and whether the figure is stock or flow. `PAGE_NUMERIC_MATCH` is never substituted for `SEMANTIC_SOURCE_VERIFIED`. A shifted physical-page citation or a ten-thousand-yuan unit error blocks the affected chart.
3. **MULTIPLE EARNINGS DEFINITIONS.** Distinguish (i) PRC GAAP net profit attributable to parent, (ii) regulatory deducted/non-recurring parent profit and (iii) company-specific adjusted/non-GAAP profit. Store precise definition, reported/derived, pretax/tax/NCI treatment, share-based compensation and investment-profit adjustment. If the complete adjustment schedule is not source-verifiable, juxtapose these definitions with caveat; NEVER invent a reconciled waterfall. Kingsoft's 2026H1 adjusted income was disclosed as `108,679.68 万元`, not ordinary report-yuan.
4. **CONSOLIDATED HYBRID BUSINESS ROUTING.** Consumer/manufacturing groups can contain licensed finance subsidiaries; consolidated CFO can include member deposits and deposits/placements at financial institutions. Identify the **cash flow statement section** (inflow vs outflow), original signed reported amount, and signed contribution to CFO separately: `CFO = reported inflows − reported outflows`. A negative value within an *outflow* row means a positive CFO net effect, not a negative cash contribution. Never describe a conglomerate's consolidated CFO increase as identical to underlying product cash conversion.
5. **HIERARCHICAL AND CROSS-AXIS SEGMENT GRAMMAR.** Every category belongs to a taxonomic level: exclusive top-level product segments, parent/subsegment, geography, channel or time. Only siblings from the *same additive partition* may be summed against consolidated totals. Midea's industrial-solutions parent already includes its four reported children; adding them again is a double count. Product and geography are two views of the same total, never a sum of both views.
6. **HEDGE/TAX CLASSIFICATION GUARD.** FX impacts recorded in finance costs, financial-asset fair values, derivatives and hedge reserves/OCI are different accounting locations. A pretax derivative number cannot be directly subtracted from after-tax, after-NCI regulatory excluded parent earnings or treated as realized economic hedge effectiveness. Build a separate signed disclosure ledger before commenting on protection and recurring profit.
7. **MOBILE AND LEGIBILITY BLOCKER.** At widths 1440/768/390, audit complete document scroll width, long SHA/URLs, Chinese heading orphans, SVG text legibility, contrasts, links and every exhibit. A chart may scroll *inside* its own figure, but its key values and first-order conclusion must be independently visible on mobile without requiring horizontal swiping. Long unbroken source fingerprints require `overflow-wrap:anywhere` or equivalent.
8. **BROWSER CONTEXT IS PART OF THE CLAIM.** Browser `set_content` validates layout/JS only; it does NOT establish distinct-file route serving, anchor navigation across actual HTTP routes, Cloudflare response identity or production deployment. Test each separately. Runtime policy blocking file/localhost navigation must record `ACTUAL_ROUTE_NOT_CHECKED`, never PASS.
9. **EVIDENCE-ADVERSARIAL REVIEW.** For every figure: precise literal PDF page, numerator and denominator, stock/flow, positive/negative sign basis, partial/full reconciliation, competing hypothesis, accessible data table, mobile first-order values and a qualified conclusion. Missing source=unknown, never zero. Reject generic graph quotas and repeating charts that answer the same question.
10. **PERSISTENT INTER-CONVERSATION HANDOFF.** The repository root `PROJECT_STATE.md` is the authoritative handoff pointer, updated at the end of each verified iteration with Git SHA, current Skill blob SHA, source manifest, sample status, local/remote gate states, unresolved P0/P1 issues, current deliverable paths, exact reproduction commands and next action. A new chat must fetch that file and the **latest** main/working-branch Skill before claiming project understanding. Do not rely on old chat summary or a temporary ZIP alone.

**Observed v8.1-RC1 tests:** 3 original files verified by SHA, 92/92 selected amounts matched to correct PDF physical pages, 11/11 targeted arithmetic checks, 20 original SVG figures in independent prototype HTML, Chromium render-only viewport tests at 1440/768/390 with zero page overflow and JS errors. **Still NOT_CHECKED:** complete audited three-statement and CFO indirect-note reconstruction, independent analyst/editor, production route HTTP, live Cloudflare serving, full visual critique of all figures as a publication, 8–12Q market/valuation data. This upgrade cannot be approved to v8.1 release merely from these counts.

## Cross-sector generalization must be real, not a generic template (v8.2-RC1)

**MANDATORY REFERENCE:** Read [05_CROSS_SECTOR_GENERALIZATION.md](research/05_CROSS_SECTOR_GENERALIZATION.md) in addition to Research 01–04. This is a distinct layer governing the actual economic model of each issuer, and must drive whether each figure is permitted.

**Universal proof kernel + typed sector adapters.** First determine the issuer's reported business models, consolidation perimeter, ownership, regulated subsidiaries, accounting regime, disclosures and core risks. Choose the primary model and zero or more material overlays. Domain profiles include manufacturing, software/platform, consumption/retail, commercial banks, insurers, insurance/bank holding groups, real estate developers, securities/asset management, pharma, construction, utilities and conglomerates. An industry in the routing catalog is NOT automatically an implemented real-data analytical and publication capability.

**Typed source graph.** Every fact must preserve physical page and original label, exact unit and currency, stock versus flow and date/period, accounting standard, report revision, consolidated/parent/subsidiary reporting scope, whether it is disclosed/derived/management-adjusted, signed original presentation, denominator and calculation. `营业收入` versus `营业总收入`, bank NII, gross premium versus insurance service revenue, recognized revenue versus backlog/presales and GAAP net versus company operating earnings are distinct field definitions. Missing is unknown, never zero.

**Industry financial quality contracts.** Bank: interest income minus interest expense, NIM, loan ECL/NPL, loan quality stages, deposits, RWA and CET1; reject industrial COGS/FCF interpretation. Insurer: insurance service + insurance finance result, contractual service margin roll-forward, claims, actuarial sensitivity, asset/liability duration, solvency ratios; reject premium as recognized services and CSM stock as flow. Developer: property delivery recognition, inventories and impairments, restricted cash, contracts, maturity ladder, review/audit going-concern qualification; reject presales=recognized revenue and headline monetary funds=free cash. Mixed insurer/bank: parent profit + NCI, ownership and intra-group elimination; separate management operating metrics from statutory profits. Other sectors follow their own specified accounting contracts, never the industrial default.

**High-priority audit/review finding gate.** Scan the actual audit or review report and relevant note for emphasis of matter, going-concern material uncertainty, scope limitation, audit review/no-audit distinction, restatements, legal contingencies, covenant and liquidity. A material going-concern matter must appear in the principal research narrative and risk visual, not only in the source appendix.

**Evidence-to-figure policy.** No fixed chart quota. Same-period only for additive financial flow; assets/liabilities use date-specific stocks; nonexclusive and hierarchical segments cannot be stacked together; signed totals and minority profit require exact bridges. The visual type is chosen by analytical relation, not sector logo/theme. On mobile, direct answer visible without swiping while only the complex SVG itself may scroll horizontally.

**Generalization certification, no inflated claims:** Level 0 = planned domain only; Level 1 = one real original-PDF verified issuer with sector figures; Level 2 = two materially different real issuers with complete sector statements/core note semantic accounting QA and audited or reviewed period comparisons; Level 3 = cross-domain mixed-regime cases, external financial editorial audit and true served-URL browser QA. **Three first implemented common financial relations (NOT 22 implementation claims):**

## Pharma and utilities: cross-period financial publishing contract (v8.2-RC3)

Do not make strong causal or valuation conclusions from two H1 snapshots. `period_comparison` is a fourth **implemented** basic figure grammar requiring 2025H1 and 2026H1 originally reported flows, exact source pages, normalized units and explicit comparison scope. A period-comparison chart is not an 8–12-quarter series or proof of normalized earning power. Restatement and consolidation changes require a separate semantic reviewer before publication approval.

**Pharma adapter:** separate core recognized medicine sales, innovative-drug sales, licensing/royalty/milestone revenues and contract performance obligations, one-off collaboration receipts, revenue and cash recognition, expenses vs capitalized development and clinical/regulatory risk. A line of licensing revenue should not be called sustainable medicine sales. Do not assign 2026 operating-cash decline to R&D or receivables without the signed indirect CFO and cash timing notes.

**Hydro utility adapter:** reported revenue, applicable generation/dispatch/sold-electricity denominators, tariffs and regulated rules, seasonal hydrology, maintenance vs construction CapEx, and capital returns. The period's electricity generation volume need not equal the revenue-recognized electricity sales volume, and revenue growth does not identify a tariff change.

## Editorial release-class gates: regression correction, 2026-11-02

Every published page must have an explicit truthful artifact class: FIGURE_PROTOTYPE, RESEARCH_PREVIEW, or INSTITUTIONAL_PUBLICATION. A 200 response, no overflow, valid PDF SHA or a successful 21-viewport test does NOT establish researched conclusions. An isolated one-chart company page is a FIGURE_PROTOTYPE and must NEVER be promoted as a company research article. Describing it as a beta does not make it satisfy the research mandate.

RESEARCH_PREVIEW demands actual company-specific economic model, multiple independent decision-relevant analytical questions including earnings quality, sector-specific cash and capital risks, material opposite explanation, next disclosure falsifiers and per-figure original PDF evidence. This is not a graph count quota. Financial firms use banking/insurance-native statement metrics, not industrial margins/CFO proxies. If a key risk is an auditor's material going-concern uncertainty, it belongs in the lead research narrative.

INSTITUTIONAL_PUBLICATION also requires full relevant 3-statements-plus-notes evidence, semantic review beyond literal page amount matching, exact signed accounting bridges, second financial reviewer, chart-by-chart editorial critique, actual browser route interaction and public response identity. Missing gates remain FAIL or NOT_CHECKED. Never promote a weaker artifact to a stronger class to meet delivery expectations.

Real visual review cannot be replaced by screenshot file counts. Actual earlier Ubuntu Chromium screenshots of seven one-chart reports showed Chinese text rendered as empty squares: font assets were missing from the CI browser image, while the width/JS assertions still passed. Install a CJK font in CI, inspect real screenshots and test content legibility and narrative quality. Cloudflare can canonicalize /article.html to /article and /index.html to /directory/; accept both in URL navigation assertions, then verify the destination contents, not only the string.

Observed source regression: seven one-figure prototypes were promoted to the homepage while earlier three-issuer 20-figure source-backed editorial studies were ignored. The corrective main release now features these three RESEARCH_PREVIEW pages in public/research/2026-h1 and demotes one-chart pages to a clearly labeled technical research-lab directory. ## Required operating sequence

1. **Intake:** confirm exact PDF source SHA, issuer, ticker, report version/revision, report period, audited status, currency, scale, stock/flow, consolidated vs parent, comparative and restatement. Map real PDF physical pages to report printed pages. Fail closed when mismatch.
2. **PDF-to-evidence:** read business/MD&A/risk, all three consolidated statements, product/geography/segment notes, revenue policy, receivables/contract assets, inventory and provisions, cash supplement, R&D, CapEx/CIP, related parties, share incentives, nonrecurring, guarantees and audit. PDF parsers merely nominate values; a human/source-review artifact verifies fragmented tables. Record original line and page.
3. **Evidence ledger:** exact signed Decimal yuan; date or period; consolidation; source label/page/sha; calculation proof; confidence and reviewer. The only allowed estimate without source is a clearly labeled illustrative calculation, not presented as a historical fact. Missing cannot become zero.
4. **Industry route:** identify manufacturing, semiconductor, pharma, bank, insurer, broker, developer, utility, platform etc. Banks/insurance must not use industrial COGS/FCF and should fail closed until a suitable specialist template is available.
5. **Analyst memo:** economic model, three alternative thesis candidates, magnitude analysis of revenue+GP+expenses, recurring profit vs reported, cash conversion, working capital/counterparty, reinvestment/return on capital, key market/technology risk, strongest opposing hypothesis, evidence gaps, 3–5 testable follow-ups. Label every assertion as DISCLOSED / MANAGEMENT / DERIVED / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN.
6. **Chart selection (NO chart quota):** select graphic by the analytic relationship. Conserved nonnegative allocation uses Sankey; signed terms use waterfalls; balance composition is a stock identity; revenue and margins trend require comparable periods; peer/valuation need independent authorized market/peer data. A single original PDF cannot justify 8–12Q trends or stock targets.
7. **Visual editorial:** publish thesis first, strong information hierarchy, large primary visual, direct amount and pp labels, compact close-to-chart sources, counterview, analytical watchlist; deliberate whitespace and typographic consistency. Avoid mass-production dashboard cards, pseudo-Sankey and decorative graphs. Mobile SVG charts may scroll **inside** the chart with an explicit hint, never overflow the whole page.
8. **Accounting gates:** exact ledger balance sheet, GP bridge, P&L signed subtotal, minority interests, net income + all individual CFO adjustments, cash close identity, reported vs derived segment residual, source count, matching periods. Any material fail blocks affected figures.
9. **Red-team:** a financial/casual mechanism that could mimic the observed data; unsourced peer claims; unreported ASP/yield; AR concentration misnamed revenue share; monetary funds vs cash equivalents; stock changes mistaken for CFO adjustments; FX P&L mistaken for cash FX; false net-zero from missing inputs.
10. **Browser and publication:** playwright Chromium 1440/768/390, no page-level horizontal overflow, no JS errors/network dependency, figures readable, accessible keyboard tooltips and SVG export, all links anchored, static screenshot inspection and 5-second graphic critique. Check GitHub blob and public Pages HTTP separately; do not claim live if only GitHub is verified. User-authorized repo only, preview before production.
