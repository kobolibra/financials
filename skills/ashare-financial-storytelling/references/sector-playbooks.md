# Sector Routing — choose the correct economics, never force a universal Sankey

**Common initial questions:** how does the company earn money, who pays, what constrains volume/pricing/margin, and what working capital/capex is needed? Use the report's segment notes and industry disclosures to route the analysis.

| Sector / situation | Priority economics | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| Optical components, electronics, high-speed connectivity | product level GP; passive/active mix; process yield **if disclosed**; key materials and supply tightness; R&D, automation, Thai/domestic expansion; customers and foreign currency; AR and raw inventory | do not infer shipments, 800G/1.6T market share, price-volume, capacity utilization, or identifiable customers without source |
| Semiconductors | foundry/capacity, utilization/yield, ASP/product generation if reported; inventory impairment, R&D capitalization, customer concentration, subsidies | wafer shipments and industry ASP do not establish issuer unit economics without mapping |
| Machinery & industrial | orders/backlog if audited and consistently defined; contract liabilities/assets, receivable collection, warranty, capacity, labor, raw materials | backlogs may be nonbinding or revenue recognized later |
| Commodity & energy | production/sales price vs cost curve; hedges, reserves/life, commodity cycle; safety/environmental capex | mark-to-market gains and commodity price changes should not be blindly annualized |
| Consumer/retail | store count, SSS growth, channel inventory, gross margin, distribution, returns, loyalty if disclosed | channel pull-forward and distributor accounting; quarter seasonality |
| Pharmaceutical/biotech | patented portfolio, R&D trial milestones, licensing/revenue recognition, commercialization costs, cash runway | pre-commercial firms lack conventional revenue gross-margin economics |
| Software/platform | recurring revenues, customer retention, monetization, CAC, SBC if disclosed, contract liabilities | do not invent ARR, churn, GMV, user cohort metrics |
| Utilities/telecom operators | tariffs, MWh/subscriber/ARPU, regulatory return, network capex, debt maturities | long asset lives and regulated prices require different return standards |
| Property/developers | presales to recognized revenue, inventory/project completion, contract liabilities, funding maturities, cash restrictions | presale != recognized revenue or CFO; avoid simplistic current ratio |
| Banks | NIM, earning assets, deposits, credit risk cost, NPL/coverage, capital adequacy, liquidity | industrial COGS/FCF, general CFO quality tests inapplicable; loan book not an inventory |
| Insurers | premiums, CSM/contract liabilities as applicable, investment spread, solvency, claims, investment duration | no ordinary gross-margin Sankey; reporting standard differences matter |
| Brokers, AM, financial services | assets under management, fee rate, transactions, investment income, leverage, risk controls | non-consolidated AUM is not on-balance assets |

Always inspect issuer-specific disclosure under applicable exchange industry guidance. If the PDF lacks a KPI, say "not disclosed"; either exclude chart or supply a **clearly labeled** external data appendix only if outside research is approved. Sector peer comparisons require like-for-like accounting and business mix.
