# Track B — Independent institutional A-share financial-statement research architecture
Research date: 2026-10-30. This document is independent of AEI graphics; it drives content quality for PRC filings. Applies only after identifying board, sector, document period, accounting version and audited status.

## Primary regulatory and accounting authorities (date-sensitive)
- [CSRC listed-company 2025 annual-report content and format standard](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c101954/c7547588/content.shtml): effective 2025-07-01. It governs annual filing content; do **not** assume all provisions equally apply to unaudited H1.
- [CSRC Information Disclosure Measures](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c106256/c1653948/content.shtml): annual audits and periodic-report requirements.
- [CSRC 2025 annual financial-report accounting regulatory report, released 2026-08-14](https://www.investor.org.cn/zczx/qwzx/zjhfb/202608/t20260817_927998.shtml): recurring identified issues in revenue, financial instruments, business combinations, impairments and R&D. These are **population-level regulatory concerns, not evidence any specific issuer is wrong**.
- [CSRC Nonrecurring Gains/Losses 2023 interpretation](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c101954/c7451398/content.shtml): current-period classification and adjustments including tax/minority interests.
- [SZSE ChiNext industry information disclosure guideline No.4 (2023 edition)](https://docs.static.szse.cn/www/lawrules/rule/stock/supervision/W020230210725777711333.pdf): special disclosure for communications including overseas sales exposure, technology/R&D, inventory write-down and receivables allowances. Confirm successor/current version before declaring compliance.
- [SZSE regulatory/disclosure guidance PDF](https://www.csf.com.cn/publish/main/1040/1043/20240524151520092597256/1716534934251.pdf): useful periodic-report interpretation but ensure document's current applicability.
- Actual issuer report PDF and notes **take priority for what issuer states**, with original fingerprint and physical-to-printed page mapping; compare amendments and restatements.

## Ten analyst workstreams
1. **Company's value chain and income mechanism.** Products and services, contribution, delivery/recognition policy, capacities and capacity utilization (only when disclosed), pricing power claims and evidence, contractual liabilities, supply concentration and customer dependencies.
2. **Accounting basis and comparability.** Consolidated vs parent, CAS sector, report revisions and audited H1, comparable period, balance stocks as of fiscal year-end vs current balance; EPS adjustment for share conversions; FY/H1/Q3-YTD isolation.
3. **Growth quality.** Revenue and GP by segment/geography/channel with denominator & gross/NET unit; compute factual segment changes and contribution; separately analyze volume, ASP, mix, FX, acquisitions and timing only with reliable inputs. Never claim “AI demand caused growth” as a verified decomposition from issuer MD&A alone.
4. **Profit sustainability.** COGS, gross margin, labor/material/capacity if disclosed, R&D (expense vs capitalization), share compensation, asset/credit loss, fair value/investment income, subsidies, tax, minority interest, attributable & nonrecurring-adjusted profit. Use exact P&L sign structure.
5. **Cash earnings and working capital.** Full signed indirect CFO reconciliation; operating receivables/payables and inventory adjustments not equal to BS change; cash realization, receivable aging, ECL, bills, factoring, contract assets, restricted cash, inventory stages/impairments, prepaid supplier concentration. Convert p&l revenue to cash only where properly evidenced.
6. **Capital allocation & return.** Cash CapEx and maintenance/growth differentiation if evidenced; projects CIP→PPE and timing; debt maturities, financing rates, dividend/buyback cash, treasury, equity issuance, SBC/dilution, deferred tax; ROE with weighted equity and ROIC only with consistent capital basis.
7. **Forensic footnote review.** Top-five concentration denominators (AR is not sales; prepaid supplier is not purchase); related parties, guarantees, pledged assets, capitalized interest/R&D, government grants, unearned revenue, unbilled receivables, non-standard audits, extraordinary costs and post-report events. Red flags trigger questioning rather than allegations.
8. **Sector operating model.** Industrial electronics/optical; semiconductor; consumer retail; drugs/biotech; software/platform; utilities; commodity; construction/property; banks; insurers; brokers. Each has different ratios and disclosures. Fail closed where no sector model exists, rather than forcing manufacturing COGS/FCF on banks.
9. **Risk & alternatives.** Explicit downside mechanism, base thesis, rival explanations, both supportive and contradictory facts, source certainty. Scenarios are conditional with named assumptions, never unsourced probabilities.
10. **Monitoring & research memo.** Five falsifiable indicators with current sourced anchors, next disclosure/date if known, direction to watch, implication. Unanswered material questions, target items for next filing, and what data could settle competing hypotheses.

## Mandatory three-layer evidence
**Fact record**: exact yuan Decimal, raw label, PDF + printed page, period and currency, consolidated/parent, stock/flow, original PDF SHA, reported/derived, formula, confidence, source visual/table snippet where feasible. Null/unverified never coerced to zero.
**Claim record**: verbatim or short attributable issuer statement, exact page; classify as issuer management, regulator, independent evidence, analyst inference or unknown.
**Research inference**: claim IDs + calculations + competing mechanism + why inference survives/doesn't survive; include disconfirming disclosure. Infer from document rather than “fill in” missing data.

## Deterministic accounting QC
- A = L + E incl minorities; cash open + CFO+CFI+CFF+cash FX = cash close, same scope.
- Income line sequence and signs: Revenue−total op cost + other signed items→operating profit→pretax→consolidated net income→parent + minority; never assume one line contains all expenses.
- Gross profit = revenue − COGS where industry applicable. Don't mix segment dimension overlaps or turn mathematical residual into undisclosed business.
- Net income + **every signed disclosed indirect line** = CFO. Missing line blocks cash bridge, not arbitrary "misc".
- CFO − cash spent acquiring/constructing LT assets = analyst-defined simple FCF; coverage and comparability caveats.
- Compare H1 to previous H1 flows, 6/30 to preceding 12/31 stocks; prior comparatives restated where required. Weighted ROE and adjusted EPS as disclosed.
- Distinguish monetary funds from cash equivalents, profit FX from cash FX from OCI translation.
- Numbers near zero: percent moves misleading; absolute magnitude and materiality matter. All ratios carry numerator/denominator, fiscal date and definition.
- Reporting PASS/FAIL/NOT_CHECKED per applicable identity; never divide by zero or hide NOT_CHECKED.

## Institutional investment note required outputs
A. Company economics map and explicit confidence level.
B. Executive investment-research thesis (not necessarily a buy/sell recommendation).
C. Quantified operating growth/profit bridges with counterfactual hypothesis.
D. Earnings quality and accrual/working capital/impairment detail.
E. Liquidity, financing, capital deployment and returns.
F. Notes-forensics risk heatmap with evidence and false-positive limitations.
G. Industry operating indicators, appropriately disclosed only.
H. Explicit what-could-go-wrong and monitoring sheet.
I. Sources, definitions, audit/revision status, conflicts and not-known.

## Materiality and anti-overclaim gates
Investigate unusually large or thesis-relevant changes (e.g. >10% as **heuristic, not statutory cut-off**). More source lines do not inherently equal more depth; significance, explanatory power and independent disconfirmation do. A top-five AR share is not revenue concentration. A company saying its global AI opportunity is large is not independent TAM proof. Unaudited H1 is not audited FY. One company's case does not validate general-purpose automatic extraction.
