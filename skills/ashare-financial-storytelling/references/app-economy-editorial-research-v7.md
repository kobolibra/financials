## What to emulate — as design principles, not assets
- **Business mechanism first**: each chart answers a concrete question about operations; opening should be a two-sentence analyst view, not marketing.
- **Accounting-preserving flow diagrams**: flow band thickness represents amount, each node conserved; show segment revenue→cost/GP and cash movement at different levels, with exact units and source note.
- **Integrated labels**: mix %, margins, YoY and annotation adjacent to relevant nodes; no compulsory tooltip for essential data.
- **Multi-view grammar**: statement flow≠time series≠multiple dispersion≠peer scatter; every analytic claim picks a correct visual family.
- **Editorial hierarchy**: evidence-backed one-line takeaway, large focal figure, short adjacent context, subtle note; consistent dark/light/semantic cost/earnings palette and typography chosen for legibility, **not a copy of AEI fonts/colors**.
- **Visual audit**: check at original screenshot size and narrow mobile that readers can understand every figure without zoom; 5-second takeaway; explain uncertainty in the chart, not only footer.
- **Selection discipline**: missing comparable history -> show explicit DATA GAP, NEVER chart a fictitious trend, market price or peer.
- **Reader agency**: rival explanation and what would change the thesis; opportunities and risks given comparable weight.
