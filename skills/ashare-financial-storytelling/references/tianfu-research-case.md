## Distinct hypotheses, not one neat bullish story
H1 (positive): favorable passive mix and within-product process/gross margin improvement produce unusual GP leverage.
H2 (challenge): product price/input changes, material shortage, customer order composition and temporary utilization effects could explain much of the same outcome. Not quantifiable from PDF without comparable volume/ASP.
H3 (balance): rapid raw-material build may enable delivery under shortages OR may increase the risk of obsolescence and cash lock-up; raw/stock data alone cannot prove either.
H4 (cash): CFO improved but underlying working-capital requirements are substantial. Do not equate CFO improvement with full realized cash economics of every earned yuan.
H5 (concentration): top-5 AR exposure merits customer/credit review; not evidence of 84% revenue concentration or of bad debt.

## Research follow-up and falsifiers
- Does passive device GM stay elevated across FY26 and future quarters, and why? Need comparable product COGS, ASP/shipments and yield/capacity; missing metrics flagged.
- Does active product revenue and throughput recover when supply conditions ease? Management statements, next product reporting.
- Is inventory mainly strategic raw materials or slow moving? Track raw/WIP/finished net amounts and impairment provisions next report.
- Is receivable balance growth ultimately collected? Follow aging, ECL, top-5 AR share and subsequent cash collection.
- Will Thai ramp/expansion increase returns more than incremental CapEx / working capital? Need period-comparable utilization, capex, revenue by facility and maintenance/growth CapEx split not disclosed.
